## Workshop change notes

When asked for change notes for the Steam Workshop, keep them simple and player-facing:
only what got fixed or changed in the game. No tests, tooling, code or internals.
One bullet per change, for example:

- Fixed: Sea Animal Management Plan (#138). Supporting its left slot (2 CP + reef icon) now carries out the reef effects of your played sea animals. Before, the game stopped with an error while giving out the rewards.
